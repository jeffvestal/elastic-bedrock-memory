Every AI Agent
Starts with Amnesia

By default, AI agents are stateless. Each conversation begins at zero. Your agent has no memory of what it built yesterday, what decisions were made last sprint, or why the team chose Aurora PostgreSQL.

The common workaround — stuffing everything into the context window — breaks at enterprise scale. It's slow. It's expensive. And it still has a ceiling.
